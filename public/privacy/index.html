THE SHORT VERSION

Your table stays yours.

Tableclock has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Player names, preferences, and an unfinished clock are stored only in your browser.

Room sync

If you choose room sync where it is enabled, the current clock state and player names pass ephemerally through the relay. Rooms are not archived. Use nicknames if you prefer.

Your controls

Export a setup as JSON, clear a game from the menu, or remove all data by clearing this site’s storage in your browser.
